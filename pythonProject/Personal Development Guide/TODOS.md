Here’s a practical, no-BS list of things every man living in an apartment society (especially in India) should know / be good at:

1. **Know your society bye-laws** – parking rules, timing of water pump, pet policy, noise timings, guest parking charges, etc.

2. **How to read your electricity & water meter** – take photo on 1st of every month. Prevents surprise bills.

3. **Basic plumbing fixes** – change washer in tap, unclog sink with plunger + caustic soda, close main valve when leaving for long.

4. **How to prime the water pump** – most common reason society maintenance guy charges ₹300–500.

5. **Where is your society DG backup connected?** – which lights/fans/sockets work on inverter & which on DG.

6. **How to reset MCB / change a fuse** – and which MCB controls which room.

7. **Basic electrical safety** – never touch wet switch, never overload extension board, keep MCB rating in mind.

8. **How to use fire extinguisher** – PASS method (Pull, Aim, Squeeze, Sweep). Most societies have one per floor.

9. **Neighbor communication basics** – smile + namaste > confrontation. Small talk buys you a lot of goodwill.

10. **Society politics 101** – don’t take sides in the first 2 years. Listen more, opine less.

11. **How to complain effectively** – written email to secretary + CC facility manager + photos + polite language > verbal shouting.

12. **Know your flat’s layout & measurements** – useful for buying furniture, AC, geyser, etc.

13. **Basic tool kit** – hammer, adjustable spanner, screwdriver set, pliers, tape, quick-fix epoxy, WD-40.

14. **Emergency numbers saved** – society security, plumber, electrician, lift company, nearby hospital.

15. **How to shut main gas & water valve** – in 10 seconds when something goes wrong.

16. **Parking etiquette** – don’t block, don’t scratch, don’t park in visitor slot “just for 5 min”.

17. **Lift manners** – don’t hold door too long, don’t fart (obvious but very common complaint).

18. **Know your RWA election cycle** – sometimes worth standing or at least voting.
