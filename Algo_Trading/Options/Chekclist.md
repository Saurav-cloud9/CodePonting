## 🎯 Nifty Options Theta Trade Checklist

### ✅ **1. DTE Timing**
- [ ] **5-7 DTE** (Mon-Wed of expiry week)
- [ ] Avoid: <3 DTE (gamma zone)
- [ ] Avoid: >8 DTE (theta too slow)

### ✅ **2. Market Structure**
- [ ] Nifty in **defined range** for 3+ days (not testing extremes)
- [ ] Entry **after** range confirmation, not at highs/lows
- [ ] No major events scheduled (Fed, Budget, RBI) within 3 days

### ✅ **3. Strike Selection**
- [ ] Strikes **above** nearest major support (NOT below)
- [ ] Distance: **150-250 pts OTM** from current price
- [ ] Check:  Breakeven < nearest support level

### ✅ **4. OI Analysis**
- [ ] Major support OI **above** your short strike (not at/below)
- [ ] Avoid strikes with **>50L OI** (cascade risk)
- [ ] PCR at support level:  **>1.2** (bullish bias)
- [ ] Weekly vs Monthly OI aligned (no divergence >20%)

### ✅ **5. Greeks Validation**
- [ ] Expected theta: **₹400-600/day** (3 lots)
- [ ] Vega exposure: **<₹600** (IV spike risk managed)
- [ ] Delta: **<0.3** per leg (low directional risk)
- [ ] Gamma: **<0.05** (manageable acceleration)

### ✅ **6. Risk Management**
- [ ] Max loss defined: **<₹20,000** (spread width × lots × 65)
- [ ] Stop-loss level: **Nearest support -50 pts**
- [ ] Position size: **<15% margin** utilization
- [ ] Exit plan documented before entry

### ✅ **7. Entry Execution**
- [ ] Entry time: **10 AM - 2 PM** (avoid opening/closing volatility)
- [ ] Spread filled as **single order** (not legs separately)
- [ ] Premium collected: **₹80-120** (historical avg for 200pt OTM spread)

### ✅ **8. Exit Triggers (Set Alerts)**
- [ ] **Profit target**: 50% gain OR Monday 2-3 PM (whichever first)
- [ ] **Stop-loss**: Support level -50 pts breaks
- [ ] **Time-based**: Friday 2 PM auto-exit (gamma risk)
- [ ] **OI alert**: Support level OI drops >15% in 1 day

## 🚨 **Red Flags (DO NOT TRADE)**
- [ ] Nifty at 52-week high/low
- [ ] VIX >18 (high volatility environment)
- [ ] Support OI <20L (weak floor)
- [ ] Major event <3 days away
- [ ] Personal FOMO/revenge trading mindset
